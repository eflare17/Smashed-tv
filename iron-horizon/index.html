A railroad empire · 1835 – 1950
Lay track between towns and industries, build stations, buy locomotives and set their routes. Haul passengers, mail and freight along supply chains — coal and ore to the steel mill, steel and lumber to the factory, goods to the cities. Reach a net worth of $5 million by 1880.
